QA to QA agencies. **They are not legal advice.** Have a lawyer read the terms, privacy policy and
data processing agreement before the first paid contract (a few hours of review: budget roughly
₹10,000 to 30,000). Fill in every `[bracket]`.

## Decisions only you can make

- **Business form.** A sole proprietorship costs nothing to start (a current account in the
  business name, Udyam registration is free). A private limited company costs about
  ₹10,000 to 15,000 to register and more to run each year (accounts, filings), but separates your
  personal money from the business. Start as a proprietor; move when revenue or a client needs it.
- **GST.** Registration is mandatory once yearly turnover from services passes ₹20 lakh (lower in
  some special-category states); check the current rules with a CA. Many
  agencies will ask for a GST invoice so they can claim input credit, so registering early can win
  deals even below the limit. The invoice template has both versions.
- **Your legal name, address and contact** go into every document. The website's privacy page
  never shows a phone number; keep it that way.
- **Jurisdiction**: the drafts say the courts of `[your city]` and Indian law.
