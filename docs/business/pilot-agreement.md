**Between** [Your legal name / business name], [address] ("Nightshift QA"), and
[Agency legal name], [address] ("the Agency").

## 1. What the pilot is
- 30 days from [start date], free of charge. Either side can end it early by email.
- Up to 3 of the Agency's staff accounts, and [one] client project chosen by the Agency.
- Nightshift QA sets up the first tests with the Agency, runs them nightly and on request, and
  sends a client report after each run.
- Success is measured against the pilot plan agreed at the start ([link or attach]): for example
  hours of manual regression saved, real bugs caught, and false alarms.

## 2. What the Agency does
- Only points Nightshift at websites it owns or has its client's written permission to test
  (see the Acceptable Use rules), preferably a staging site, never with real payment details.
- Uses test accounts and fake data in tests. Gives test passwords only through the app's Secrets
  page (they are encrypted there), never in a test's text or by email.
- Checks every failure report before passing it to its client during the pilot: the tool is new
  to its sites.

## 3. Data
- What the Agency puts in (tests, test accounts, its clients' site addresses) and what runs
  produce (screenshots, reports) belong to the Agency.
- Nightshift QA uses it only to run the Agency's tests and support the pilot, and never sells or
  shares it. Page screenshots are sent to the AI model provider named in the security overview
  to decide each step.
- At the end of the pilot, or whenever the Agency asks, Nightshift QA deletes the Agency's
  workspace and its data within 7 days. Backups that still hold it age out within 14 days.

## 4. No promises beyond best effort
The pilot is free and on a best-effort basis: no guaranteed uptime, and Nightshift QA is not
liable for anything that follows from a missed or wrong test result. Neither side owes the other
anything when the pilot ends, unless they sign a paid plan.
