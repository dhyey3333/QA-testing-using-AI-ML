# Data Processing Agreement

*Draft, not legal advice. Part of the Terms of Service between [Your legal name / business name]
("the Processor") and [Agency legal name] ("the Agency"). Written with India's Digital Personal
Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) in mind; have a lawyer check it, and adapt it if a client is
under GDPR.*

## 1. Roles
For personal data inside the Agency's tests and test results ("Agency Data"), the Agency (or its
client) decides why and how it is processed (the Data Fiduciary). The Processor processes it only
on the Agency's behalf, to provide the Service.

## 2. What is processed
- **Purpose:** running, recording and reporting the Agency's website tests and explorations.
- **Data:** test accounts and test values, website addresses, page text and screenshots captured
  during runs, reports. The Agency should use fake or test data; real customers' personal data
  should not be put into tests.
- **People it may concern:** the Agency's staff, and whoever appears on the pages under test.

## 3. The Processor's commitments
1. Process Agency Data only to provide the Service and on the Agency's documented instructions
   (these terms and its use of the app).
2. Keep it confidential, and give access only to people who need it to run the Service and are
   bound to confidentiality.
3. Protect it with reasonable security safeguards, at least: encryption of secrets at rest,
   HTTPS in transit, hashed passwords, optional two-factor login, workspace isolation between
   agencies, an activity log, and nightly backups (see the security overview).
4. Use only the sub-processors listed in section 5, and tell the Agency at least 14 days before
   adding or replacing one, so it can object.
5. Tell the Agency without undue delay, and within 72 hours of becoming aware, of any personal
   data breach affecting Agency Data, with what is known and what is being done, so the Agency
   can meet its own duties to inform the Data Protection Board and affected people.
6. Help the Agency answer requests from people exercising their rights, as far as the Service
   allows (the Agency can view, change and delete its data in the app).
7. Delete Agency Data when the Agency deletes it in the app, and all of it within 30 days of the
   contract ending; backups age out within 14 days after that.
8. Make available what the Agency reasonably needs to check these commitments (this document,
   the security overview, answers to its questionnaire).

## 4. The Agency's commitments
The Agency has the right to give the Processor this data and to have the websites tested, uses
test data where it can, and keeps its own staff's passwords and devices secure.
