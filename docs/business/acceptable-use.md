# Acceptable Use rules

*Part of the Terms of Service. Draft, not legal advice.*

Nightshift QA drives a real browser against websites. Used on the wrong site, that can look like
an attack, cost someone money or break the law. So:

1. **Only test websites you own, or that your client has given you written permission to test.**
   Keep that permission; we may ask for it. Prefer a staging copy over the live site.
2. **No real payment details, ever.** Use the payment provider's test mode and its published test
   cards or UPI IDs.
3. **Use test accounts and fake personal data** in tests. Real customers' data doesn't belong in a
   test, a secret or a report.
4. **Don't use the Service to** attack, overload or probe sites for security weaknesses you aren't
   authorised to test; get around bot protection, CAPTCHAs or rate limits; scrape content you have
   no right to; or send messages or orders that reach real people.
5. **Keep the load reasonable.** Nightly runs of a normal test suite are fine; thousands of runs an
   hour against someone's server are not.
6. **Don't share accounts.** Invite each person; their actions are logged under their name.

We may pause a project or account that breaks these rules, and will tell you why.
