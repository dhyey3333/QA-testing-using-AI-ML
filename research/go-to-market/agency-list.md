# Indian QA agencies to approach (first list, 2026-10-09)

Built from public directories and the firms' own pages (sources below). Company-level facts only,
no personal contacts: find the founder, delivery head or QA manager yourself on the company's
site or LinkedIn, as `outreach.md` says. **Every figure here is self-reported or from a
directory; check the firm's site before writing.** Sizes are from Clutch or similar where shown.

**Who fits:** an independent testing firm of roughly 10 to 250 people that sells manual and
regression testing of web apps to other companies. They feel the cost of re-testing the same
flows every release, and a nightly AI suite with a branded client report raises their margin.
**Who doesn't:** the very large (Cigniti, QA Mentor at 250 to 999 people, Capital Numbers), firms
whose main product is their own AI testing platform (they are competitors), and pure development
shops.

## How to use this list
1. Open each firm's site; note what kind of clients they serve and whether they show web-app work.
2. Find one decision maker per firm (founder, delivery head, QA manager) on the site or LinkedIn.
3. Send the LinkedIn note or cold email from `outreach.md`, 10 to 15 a day by hand, and log
   replies in a sheet (firm, person, date, reply, next step).
4. Aim: 2 pilots from the first 20. Use `docs/business/pilot-agreement.md` and `pilot-plan.md`.
