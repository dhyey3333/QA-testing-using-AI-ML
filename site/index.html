How it works

From a sentence to a nightly test suite

Your testers describe what a person does and what should happen. Nightshift QA does the clicking, every night.

1

Describe the test, or let the AI draft it

Steps and expected results go in a form, in plain English. No selectors, no code.

  • Paste a URL: the AI explores the site and drafts tests to review
  • Already on Cucumber? Import your .feature files
  • Passwords stay in write-only secrets the AI never sees
2

It runs every night, in real browsers

Schedule a nightly time per client, or press Run now before a release. Several tests run at once.

  • Chrome, Firefox and Safari, plus iPhone and Android screens
  • Passing paths are replayed with no AI: a stable suite runs in seconds for ₹0
  • When the page changes, the AI finds the new path and saves it
3

You get proof, not a green tick

Every expected result must be quoted from the page, and code checks the quote is really there.

  • Each failure is sorted: bug, flaky, outdated test, or site problem
  • The likely cause, in one sentence, from the evidence
  • A client report under your agency's name, ready to send

FAQ

Questions agencies ask

Do my testers need to code?

No. A test is a form: the steps and what should happen, in plain English. YAML is one click away for those who want it.

Which websites can it test?

Any website your client lets you test, ideally a staging or test environment. Sites with bot protection, such as large marketplaces, block automated browsers; Nightshift QA reports those as blocked instead of guessing.

How often is it wrong?

On our benchmark it caught 23 of 23 planted bugs, made no false passes in about 400 runs, and raised a false alarm on 1 in 45 clean runs of an app it was never tuned on. The pilot measures the same numbers on your project.

Does it test mobile apps?

Mobile websites, yes: iPhone and Android screen sizes, touch and browsers. Native apps from the Play Store or App Store, not yet.

What does the AI see?

Screenshots and text of the page under test, with your test data replaced by placeholders. Passwords and keys stay on the server and are never sent to the AI.

What happens after the pilot?

Pick a plan, or leave with your tests as plain text and your passing paths as standard Playwright tests.
