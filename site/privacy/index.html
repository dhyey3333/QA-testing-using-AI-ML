Privacy

What this website and the Nightshift QA app keep, what the AI sees, and for how long. Last updated 7 October 2026.

This website

These pages set no cookies and run no analytics or tracking scripts. They are hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Applying for a pilot

The pilot application is a Google Form. What you type there is stored by Google Forms and seen by us, and we use it only to reply to you about the pilot. Google's privacy policy applies to the form.

The Nightshift QA app

For agencies on a pilot or a plan, the app stores:

  • Logins: each person's email, name, and password as a slow, salted hash. We can't read your password.
  • Per client project: its tests, run results, screenshots of the site under test, and reports.
  • Secrets: test passwords and keys. They are write-only: the app never shows them again, uses them only inside the test browser, and never sends them to the AI.

Please test staging or test environments with test accounts only, and don't put real customers' personal data in tests.

What the AI sees

To run a test, the AI model receives screenshots and text of the page under test and the test's steps and expected results, with your test data replaced by placeholders such as {{email}}. It never receives passwords or keys. The model is an open-weights model run by a model provider (currently Ollama Cloud).

How long data is kept

The newest 60 runs of each project keep their screenshots and reports; older runs keep only a summary. When a pilot or plan ends, we delete your workspace on request. Your tests and reports belong to your agency, and you can export them before you leave.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: write to us through the contact form.
