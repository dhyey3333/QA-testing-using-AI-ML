The 30-day pilot

Prove it on one real client project, with your own numbers. We set it up with you, run it nightly for four weeks, and measure the result together. If it doesn't pay for itself, you walk away with your tests.

WhoOne QA agency, one client project, 1 to 3 of your testers
WhatYour client's staging or test environment, with the client's written OK. Never production with real customer data.
Tests10 to 25 regression flows you already run by hand every release: login, search, cart, checkout, forms, account pages

Four weeks, step by step

Day 0

You

Pick the project, share a staging URL and test accounts, and write down today's numbers.

We

Create your workspace and logins in a 45-minute call, and set up the secrets.

Week 1

You

Draft tests from the URL or write them in the form, then review and accept 10 to 25.

We

Join one working session and fix anything that blocks a test.

Weeks 2–4

You

Nightly runs, plus Run now before each release. Mark each failure as a real bug or not.

We

A 20-minute check-in each week. Anything the tool got wrong, we fix.

Day 30

You

Review the numbers with us and decide.

We

Write the pilot report: before and after, from your own run history.

How we measure success

Every number comes from the app's run history, not from opinions. The pilot succeeds if 4 of the 6 are met, including zero false passes.

MetricHow it's measuredTarget
Tester hours savedHours per regression pass, before and with Nightshift QA, counting review time50% less or better
Real bugs caughtFailures your tester confirmed as real bugsAt least 1 new bug, or every bug manual regression found
False alarmsFailures marked "not a bug", out of all runs5% or less
False passesYour tester re-checks 10 random passes by hand0
Nightly reliabilityNights the suite ran to the end, out of nights scheduled90% or more
AdoptionClient reports you actually sent to your client2 or more

What to have ready

  • A staging or test URL for one client, and their written OK to test it
  • Two or three test accounts (never real customer accounts)
  • Today's numbers: hours per manual regression pass, passes per month, and hours spent on client reports
